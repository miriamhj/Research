About

Hi! I am a recent master's graduate in the MMBEL lab at Oregon State Univeristy. I studied the impacts of retreating sea ice on marine mammal acoustic detections in the Chukchi Sea using passive acoustic monitoring (PAM).

At OSU, I investigated how retreating sea ice influences the seasonal occurrence of marine mammal acoustic detections in the Chukchi Sea. My long term goal is to pursue a doctoral degree in marine genetics and genomics, and I am looking for a position currently that would allow me to develop the technical expertise and research experience necessary to advance toward that goal.

Research experience

2024 – 2026

Impacts of Retreating Sea Ice on Marine Mammal Detections in the Chukchi Sea

Marine Mammal Bioacoustics & Ecology Lab / Oregon State University, Corvallis

During my master's with Dr. Kate Stafford, I analyzed nearly 4 years of PAM data to investigate how retreating sea ice influences the seasonal occurrence of bearded seals, Pacific walrus, bowhead whales, beluga whales, humpback whales, and fin whales in the Chukchi Sea. My findings revealed that bearded seal detections had a high positive correlation with sea ice presence while walrus detections had a negative correlation with sea ice presence. There appeared to be no significant cetacean acoustic overlap with ice, although sub-Arctic cetaceans are expanding northward.
